Delete account

This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

Irreversible

Deleting your account removes your vacancies, candidate profiles and contact details, all CV versions, AI Screening results, Recruiter Decisions, internal notes, activity history and user analytics. Your active subscription will be cancelled and future charges stopped. Payment documents and other records that must be retained by law may be kept for the required retention period.

To continue, type DELETE MY ACCOUNT. You will then be asked to confirm your password and email in the secure deletion flow.
